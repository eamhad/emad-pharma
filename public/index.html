About Emad Pharma

A specialty and generics company built around long-term conditions.

We work across the full arc of a medicine's life — discovery, clinical development, regulatory approval, and manufacturing at scale — with a focus on conditions people live with for years, not weeks.

Emad Pharma is a wholesale trader and exporter of healthcare products based in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh. We supply hospitals, clinics, and healthcare organisations with a broad catalogue spanning surgical and health equipment, tablets, injectables, capsules, syrups, and eye drops.

Every product we distribute is sourced from reliable manufacturers and checked against rigorous quality and safety standards before it reaches a customer. That focus on dependable sourcing, fair pricing, and consistent quality is what our regulatory and industry certifications are built on.

Research Pipeline

How a molecule becomes a medicine, stage by stage.

Every therapy we bring to market moves through the same six gates. We currently have programs active at every stage of this pathway. Scroll to see each stage light up.

Stage 1

Discovery

Target identification and early compound screening in our research labs.

Stage 2

Preclinical

Laboratory and animal studies to assess safety before human trials.

Stage 3

Phase I–II

First-in-human safety studies, then early efficacy in target patient groups.

Stage 4

Phase III

Large-scale trials confirming efficacy and monitoring for adverse effects.

Stage 5

Regulatory review

Submission and evaluation by health authorities in each target market.

Stage 6

Manufacturing & launch

Scale-up to commercial production and distribution to patients.
